## How to grade

For each item, mark **perfect** (locks it), **close**, or **not quite** (both route back to the
profile), with a terse note. Premises are claims too: if an item fails because its occasion was
implausible or off-form, mark it `bad-premise` and it gets regenerated, not folded into the profile.
